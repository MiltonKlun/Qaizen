# Sort products by name (STORY-103)

## Description

The inventory page gets a sort menu with two name orders. Other orders follow
in a later story.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given the inventory page, when the shopper picks "Name (A to Z)" in the sort menu, then the products are listed in alphabetical order.
2. Given the inventory page, when the shopper picks "Name (Z to A)" in the sort menu, then the products are listed in reverse alphabetical order.

## Out of scope

- Given the inventory page, when the shopper picks "Price (low to high)" in the sort menu, then the products are listed from the cheapest to the most expensive.
